Privacy Policy

Last updated:

("we", "us") is a student marketplace run by for the community. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your password.
  • Listing data: the titles, prices, descriptions, photos, meetup spots and WhatsApp number you enter when you post an offer or service.
  • Activity data: a count of how many times buyers tap "Chat on WhatsApp" on a listing, and which seller or PRO invited you (a referral code).
  • On your device: saved items, your chosen theme and a cached copy of the catalog, kept in your browser storage so the app works offline.
  • Technical data: our hosting and database providers may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type.

2. How we use it

  • To let you sign in and manage your listings.
  • To show listings to buyers and connect buyers with sellers on WhatsApp.
  • To remove fraudulent or prohibited listings, and to give PROs and administrators the statistics they need to run the service.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not show third-party advertising.

3. Google sign-in

We use Google Sign-In only to identify you. We request basic profile information (name, email). We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar, and we never post on your behalf. You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account security settings.

4. Who we share data with

  • Public: your listings (including photos and price) are visible to anyone who visits the site. Your WhatsApp number is released to a visitor only when they tap the chat button on your listing. Only post a number you are happy to share with buyers.
  • Service providers: Supabase (authentication and database), Cloudflare (hosting), Google (sign-in), and the image host used for photos (Supabase Storage, Cloudinary or ImgBB). They process data only to provide their services.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect users from fraud or harm.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your account and listings while your account is active. You can remove your own listings at any time. To delete your account and associated data, contact us using the details below and we will act on your request within a reasonable time. Backups and provider logs may persist briefly.

6. Security

Data is stored with reputable providers using encryption in transit and access rules that limit who can read or change records. No online service can be guaranteed 100% secure, so please keep your Google account protected.

7. Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, and object to its processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Children

The service is intended for university students and other adults. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use after an update means you accept the changes.
